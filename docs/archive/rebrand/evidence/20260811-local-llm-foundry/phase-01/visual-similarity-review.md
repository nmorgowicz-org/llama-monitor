# Token Ingot visual-similarity and trademark review

Review date: 2026-08-11

## Scope

- Compared the production SVG family against the accepted concept at
  `docs/brand/concepts/02-token-ingot.png` and the validation sheet.
- Searched repository-owned SVG/PNG marks for an exact duplicate or an existing
  product mark with the same stacked-layer/forge geometry.
- Checked the production SVG for external references, embedded raster content,
  scripts, filters, and font dependencies.

## Results

- The production family is a hand-authored redraw of the accepted Token Ingot
  direction. The copper center remains visible at native and small sizes.
- No exact duplicate or conflicting existing product mark was found in the
  repository-owned asset set.
- The geometry is intentionally generic enough to avoid claiming a unique
  industry symbol: three teal layers, copper center, and charcoal forge.
- SVG safety checks passed for all production variants; see
  `svg-safety-report.json`.

This is a basic engineering similarity review, not legal trademark clearance.
An external trademark/domain search remains a launch-owner responsibility
before public announcement; no similarity concern was found that blocks Phase 1.
