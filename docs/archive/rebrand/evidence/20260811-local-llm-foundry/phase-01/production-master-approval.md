# Token Ingot production-master approval

Approval basis: Nick explicitly selected **Token Ingot** as the brand direction
and accepted its validation sheet for use as the Local LLM Foundry brand. This
receipt records that decision for the deterministic production redraw; it does
not treat the generated concept PNG as a shippable master.

Approved master:

- `assets/brand/token-ingot.svg`
- Square `256` viewBox.
- Hand-authored geometry only; no raster, filter, font, script, or external
  reference.
- Copper center is rendered in front of the lower teal layer so the mark stays
  legible at native and small sizes.

Approved derivative policy:

- SVG variants remain editable source derivatives.
- PNG/ICO/ICNS/PWA/tray/package/social files are reproducible exports generated
  by `scripts/generate-brand-derivatives.mjs`.
- The concept artwork remains in `docs/brand/concepts/` as historical
  validation reference.
